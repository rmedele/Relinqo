FAQ

Professional where customers see it. Controlled where it matters.

Will AI send without approval?

Only when you allow it. You can review drafts, require approval for urgent leads, or keep everything manual.

Does it need my email password?

No. Gmail connects through Google's OAuth flow, so passwords are not stored in reqlinqo.

What if the AI is wrong?

It does not quote firm pricing or make business commitments. It responds quickly and gathers the details you need.

Who is this for?

Plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrical, restoration, garage doors, and similar local service businesses.
