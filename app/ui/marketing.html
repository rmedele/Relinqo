FAQ

Clear answers before you connect anything.

What should I bring to the audit?

One real lead leak: a missed call, slow email reply, old quote request, website form, after-hours text, or follow-up that slipped.

Does it send replies automatically?

Only if you enable that behavior. Most pilots start approval-first, then selectively turn on confident actions after the replies are proven.

Do I need to share my Gmail password?

No. The first briefing does not need Gmail access. If a pilot later uses Gmail, it connects through Google OAuth and Relinqo does not store your email password.

What happens to missed calls?

relinqo can text the caller, capture their reply, create a lead, and alert the owner when the job looks urgent.

Can I keep my existing business number?

Yes in many cases. You can route through a relinqo number or use carrier call forwarding so missed calls still become textable leads.

Who is this built for?

Local service teams where response speed matters: plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrical, restoration, garage doors, and similar trades.

What counts as a successful pilot?

Faster first replies, fewer missed-call dead ends, clearer quote follow-up, more booked estimates, and fewer opportunities slipping away.
