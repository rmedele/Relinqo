FAQ

Common questions.

Will the AI reply without my approval?

Only for routine leads it's confident about. Urgent or unusual leads always get flagged for your review. You can also turn on manual review for everything.

What if it gets something wrong?

The AI never quotes pricing, schedules appointments, or commits to timelines. It sends a professional first response and gathers info. You handle the actual business decisions.

Do I need to share my email password?

No. We connect through Google's official OAuth — the same way Slack or HubSpot would. No passwords stored, revocable in one click.

How does the missed-call rescue work?

You buy a tracking number through us in about 30 seconds. When someone calls and you don't answer, they get an instant text: "we got your call, what do you need?" Their reply becomes a lead in your dashboard.

Who is this for?

Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, garage doors, restoration, and similar local service businesses that get leads by email, web form, or ad platforms.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live the same day. We help you connect Gmail, tune the AI to your services, and run a test before going live.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no setup fees. Cancel from the billing page.
