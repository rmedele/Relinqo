FAQ

Clear answers before you connect anything.

Does it send replies automatically?

Only if you enable that behavior. You can keep review required, use owner approval, or let confident replies send after the undo window.

Do I need to share my Gmail password?

No. Gmail connects with Google OAuth, so relinqo does not store your email password.

What happens to missed calls?

relinqo can text the caller, capture their reply, create a lead, and alert the owner when the job looks urgent.

Who is this built for?

Local service teams where fast response matters: plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrical, restoration, garage doors, and similar trades.
