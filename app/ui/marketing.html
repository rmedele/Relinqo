FAQ

Common questions.

Will the AI reply without my approval?

Only for routine leads it's confident about. Urgent or unusual leads get flagged for your review. You can also turn on manual review for everything.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

The AI never promises pricing, schedules appointments, or commits to timelines. It sends a professional first response and gathers info. You handle the actual business decisions.

Do I need to give you my email password?

No. You can forward leads to LeadRelay, or we connect to your forms and ad platforms directly. IMAP polling is available but optional.

Who is this for?

Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, garage doors, restoration, and similar local service businesses that get leads by email, web form, or ad platforms.

How long does setup take?

We handle everything. Most businesses are live within 48 hours — we configure your intake sources, tune the AI to your services, and test it before going live.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no setup fees. Cancel anytime from your billing page.
