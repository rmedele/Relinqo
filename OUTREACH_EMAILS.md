# Relinqo Outreach Email Copy

Use these manually, one email at a time. Personalize the first sentence from the prospect tracker before sending.

## Subject Options

- Quick idea for missed quote requests
- Free 2-week lead response trial
- Can I test this on one real lead?
- Helping local service businesses reply faster

## Starter Reply Templates For Relinqo

Quote request:

```text
Hi {{name}}, thanks for reaching out to {{business}}. We can help with this. Could you send the service address, a few photos if safe, and the best number to reach you? Once we have that, we can give you the next available time and a rough quote range.
```

Scheduling:

```text
Hi {{name}}, we can get this scheduled. Please use this link to pick a time that works for you: {{booking_link}}. If this is urgent, reply with "urgent" and the best callback number.
```

Thank-you / won job:

```text
Hi {{name}}, thanks again for choosing {{business}}. We appreciate the chance to help. If anything comes up after the visit, reply here and we will take care of it.
```
